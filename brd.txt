1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose of the Document
This document outlines the complete business and software requirements for developing a Transport Management System for a pharmaceutical transportation company operating more than 700 trucks across multiple branches.
The objective of this document is to clearly define the client's operational requirements, establish a structured development roadmap, and serve as a common reference for project managers, designers, developers, testers, and stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.
Rather than continuing manual Excel-based operations, this platform will provide a centralized digital system where supervisors can capture trips, monitor vehicle movements, validate trips using GPS, improve billing accuracy, reduce operational irregularities, and generate real-time operational analytics from a unified dashboard.
1.2 Background
The client operates a large-scale pharmaceutical transportation business managing:
700+ Trucks
Multiple Branches
300–400 Trips per Day
Currently, all trip information is maintained manually through Excel sheets. This creates several operational challenges:
Trips are missed and remain unrecorded.
Revenue is lost because billable trips are not properly captured.
Manual processes create opportunities for unauthorized record manipulation.
Vehicle movements cannot be completely verified.
GPS information is not fully utilized.
Attendance and vehicle utilization are difficult to monitor.
Hidden kilometres between trips remain unidentified.
The client requires:
Supervisor Mobile Application
Admin Web Portal
The Trip Capture Module is the highest priority for Phase 1.
1.3 Business Objective
The primary objective is to digitize transportation operations while reducing manual administrative work, preventing revenue leakage, and improving operational transparency.
The system should enable the organization to:
Capture every trip digitally.
Record every vehicle movement, whether billable or not.
Prevent duplicate vehicle assignments.
Validate trips using GPS.
Compare GPS, odometer, and predefined route distances.
Detect hidden kilometres.
Improve billing accuracy.
Reduce operational irregularities.
Generate real-time analytics.
Replace Excel-based reporting.

17. Conclusion
The proposed Transport Management System is much more than a trip tracking application. It is a centralized operational control platform designed specifically for a pharmaceutical transportation company managing 700+ trucks across multiple branches.
The immediate focus is delivering the Phase 1 Trip Capture Module, where supervisors digitally open and close trips only after vehicle loading is completed, GPS validation begins automatically, and operational data is captured in real time. The finalized workflow also introduces structured billing classification, branch-based access control, attendance tracking, safe radius monitoring for vehicles, drivers, and supervisors, and a comprehensive exception monitoring framework.
The most significant technical outcome from the client discussion is the three-way distance verification model, where Fixed Route Distance, GPS Movement, and Odometer Readings are combined to estimate the most accurate travel distance. Together with hidden kilometre detection, route diversion monitoring, operational irregularity tracking, and centralized admin oversight, the platform provides a scalable digital foundation that significantly improves operational transparency, billing confidence, and overall transportation management efficiency.
