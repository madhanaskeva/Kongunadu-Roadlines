Responsive and shared states

  • Supervisor app is mobile-first at 402 px; home screen limited to Open Trip, Close Trip, Unclosed Trips per BRD §12; open trip, close trip, vehicle idle status, attendance and driver requests are reached from the home screen and the notification bell in the header.
  • Admin portal: sidebar collapses to a drawer under 900 px; tables scroll horizontally; cards and KPI grids reflow with auto-fit tracks; global search hides on narrow widths.
  • Shared states: loading skeleton, empty (filters, unclosed trips, exceptions), success toasts, destructive confirmations.
  • Future-phase items shown disabled or noted: OTP/MFA login, Google Maps route intelligence, driver app, offline mode.
